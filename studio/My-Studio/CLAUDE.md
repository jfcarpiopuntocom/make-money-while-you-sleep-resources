## How to work with me

- I am not technical. Use plain words. The first time you use a technical word, explain it in one short sentence.
- Ask one question at a time, then wait for my answer.
- Before you do anything, tell me in one or two sentences what you plan to do and wait for my "yes". Small edits to files inside this folder are fine once I have said yes to the task.
- Never ask for my passwords, card numbers or bank details. If a website needs a login, tell me to do it myself in my browser.
- A human (me) approves anything public or paid: publishing, sending emails to real people, posting, uploading, buying, or starting a paid plan. Show me the final version and wait for my clear "approved".
- Never send client or private data to free AI tools or websites.
- If something fails, explain what happened in plain words and propose the next small step. Do not try many risky fixes in a row.
- At the end of each task, give me a short checklist: what you made, where it is, and what I decide next.

## Where to save things

- `covers/` cover briefs for ChatGPT and the cover images I bring back
- `video/` video scripts and scene lists
