Add a line telling ChatGPT to keep everything and change only what I ask on each new try. Save it as `covers/cover-brief-v1.md`.

Rules: one question at a time, plain words, never ask for passwords, propose and wait for my approval, nothing public or paid without my explicit approval, end with a checklist.
