# Claude Code for Dummies (one page)

Claude Code is a window where you type to Claude in plain words. Claude can read and write the files in your studio folder. You stay the boss: it asks, you decide.

## The 4 steps (only once)

1. Open the `studio` folder of the kit.
2. Double-click `1-Install-Claude.bat` (on a Mac: `1-Install-Claude.command`). Wait until it says "Done".
3. Double-click `2-Start-My-Studio.bat` (Mac: `2-Start-My-Studio.command`).
4. The first time, log in with your Claude account and say yes when it asks if you trust the folder.

That is all the technical part. From here on, you just type.

## Saying yes or no

When Claude wants to do something, it shows a small menu. Use the arrow keys and press Enter:
- **Yes** to allow it once.
- **No** to refuse. Then type what you want instead, for example "No, first show me the draft."

Your studio is set up so Claude can create and edit files inside My-Studio without asking each time, and asks before anything else.

## How to stop

- Press **Esc** once to stop Claude in the middle of something. The work done so far is kept.
- Type `/exit` to close Claude Code.

## How to undo

Press **Esc twice** (with the typing box empty), or type `/rewind`. Pick the moment you want to go back to. Claude can put your files and the conversation back the way they were.

## How to come back tomorrow

- Double-click `3-Continue-Last-Session.bat` to continue exactly where you left off.
- Double-click `4-Pick-an-Old-Session.bat` to choose an older conversation from a list.

## The 10 phrases to type

1. `/interview` (start here: Master Prompt 1, your Experience Interview)
2. `/build-product` (Master Prompt 2: your product, booklet, sales page, emails, cover brief)
3. `/build-machine` (Master Prompt 3: your AI system and automation plan)
4. `/weekly-coach` (Master Prompt 4: 15 minutes every week)
5. `/make-cover-brief` (a brief to paste into free ChatGPT for your covers)
6. `/check-my-work` (a careful second reader checks your drafts)
7. "Explain that again in simpler words."
8. "Before you do it, show me what you will change."
9. `/compact` (when the chat gets long: Claude summarizes it and keeps going) or `/clear` (start a fresh chat on a new task)
10. `/usage` (see how much of your plan you have used)

More ready commands: `/write-emails`, `/make-pins`, `/video-script`, `/notify-me` (a pop-up when Claude needs you). Type `/` to see the full list.

## Small tricks

- **Plan first:** press **Shift+Tab** until you see "plan". Claude will only plan and show you, without changing anything. Press Shift+Tab again to go back.
- **Show a picture:** copy an image (a screenshot, a cover) and paste it into Claude with **Alt+V** on Windows, or **Ctrl+V** on a Mac. Then ask, for example, "What do you think of this cover?"
- **Make your plan last:** type `/model` and choose Sonnet for everyday drafts. Your $20 plan has a limit every few hours and a weekly limit, shared with claude.ai. If you hit it, wait for the reset or come back later.

## When you get stuck

Copy the error or the strange message, paste it to Claude, and write: "I got this. Explain it in plain words and tell me the one next step." If Claude itself will not open, paste the message into claude.ai in your browser.
