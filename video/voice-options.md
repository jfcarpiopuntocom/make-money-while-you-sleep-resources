# Voice Options

Checked in October 2026. Prices and licenses change: look at each tool's page before you rely on it.

## 1. Your own voice (recommended)
Record with a phone in a quiet room, close to your mouth. It is free, it is yours, and it builds trust. No license questions.

## 2. A text-to-speech (TTS) voice
- **ElevenLabs Free:** about 10,000 credits a month (roughly 10 minutes), but the free plan has **no commercial license**. Do not use it for narration of a product you sell. The Starter paid plan includes a commercial license (about $6 a month at the time of checking).
- **Microsoft options:** the Azure Speech free tier lists about 0.5 million characters a month. Whether the free tier allows commercial use is disputed; read Microsoft's product terms before using it for sold work. A consumer "Read Aloud" feature is not confirmed as licensed for this.
- **Local or open-source voices** on your own computer: check each model's license for commercial use.

## 3. Consent rules for cloning a voice
- Clone only your own voice, or the voice of a person who gave you written consent that says what it may be used for.
- Never imitate a known person.
- Tell viewers when a voice is synthetic if a platform asks you to.

## Backup plan
Free quotas run out. Keep a second voice tool or your own recording ready so a launch never waits on one service.
