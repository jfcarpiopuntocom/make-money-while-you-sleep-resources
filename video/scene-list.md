## Picture sources (pick one per scene)
- Your own photos or phone clips (best: you own them).
- Free stock clips: check each site's license for commercial use.
- Simple animated text cards made by Claude.
- AI-generated images: check the tool's terms, and disclose where a platform asks.

## Keep a record
For every picture or clip, write the source and license in a small list. You may need it if a platform asks.
