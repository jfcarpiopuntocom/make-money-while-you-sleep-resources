# Edit Checklist

Before you approve the video (the F5 approval step), watch it all the way through, once with sound and once without.

## Picture
- [ ] Every scene matches the script line
- [ ] Text on screen is readable on a phone
- [ ] Nothing on screen shows private data (names, emails, keys, client documents)
- [ ] Pictures and clips have rights; the list of sources is saved

## Sound
- [ ] Voice is clear; no clipped words
- [ ] Volume is even
- [ ] Music (if any) is licensed for commercial use and quieter than the voice
- [ ] Voice used is yours, or you have written consent

## Words
- [ ] Spelling checked in captions and on-screen text
- [ ] Every number is true and sourced, or labeled "example"
- [ ] No income promises, no fake reviews

## Technical
- [ ] Length close to plan (about 60 seconds)
- [ ] File size under the upload limit
- [ ] Title, description and thumbnail ready

## Publish
- [ ] Uploaded as **private** first
- [ ] You watched it again in YouTube
- [ ] You alone change it to public
