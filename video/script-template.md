# Video Script Template (60 seconds)

About 130 to 150 spoken words. Write it to be heard, not read.

- Working title: ______
- Who it is for: ______
- One promise of the video: ______

## Script
**Hook (0 to 5 s):** A question or a surprise about their problem. 
"______"

**Problem (5 to 15 s):** One situation they know. For example: "A new cook freezes on the line at 7 p.m. on a Saturday."
"______"

**Idea (15 to 40 s):** Your framework in three steps. 
1. ______ 
2. ______ 
3. ______

**First win (40 to 52 s):** One thing to try today. 
"______"

**Invitation (52 to 60 s):** One next step and where to find it. 
"______"

## Rules
- Plain words, short sentences.
- Every number is true and sourced, or labeled as an example.
- No income promises.
- Read it aloud once; cut whatever you stumble on.
