# Testimonial Request

Sent about 10 days after purchase (F6). One email, no pressure. Use only what real buyers write, with their permission. Never write or edit a testimonial yourself.

**Subject:** Quick question about [PRODUCT NAME]

Hi [FIRST NAME],

You have had [PRODUCT NAME] for a little while. I would love two lines from you:

1. What helped you most?
2. What would you change?

Honest answers help me improve it, including the critical ones.

If you are happy for me to quote you, please say so, and tell me how you would like your name shown (full name, first name and trade, or anonymous). I will only use your words with your yes.

Thank you,
[YOUR NAME]

P.S. If you found it useful and want to leave a public review where you bought it, that helps other people decide. Only if you want to.
