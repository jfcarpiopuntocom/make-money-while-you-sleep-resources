# Booklet Template (20 to 40 pages)

A short booklet that solves one problem and invites the reader to your next step. Fill each section; delete the helper lines.

## Title and subtitle
- Title: the result in plain words.
- Subtitle: who it is for and how long it takes.

## 1. The problem (1 page)
Describe the situation your reader is in right now, in their words. One short story.

## 2. Why the usual advice fails (1 page)
What most people try, and why it does not hold up.

## 3. The framework (4 to 8 pages)
Name it. Then one short section for each step:
- What it is
- Why it matters
- What to do (3 bullets)
- A common mistake

## 4. One worked example (3 to 5 pages)
Take one real or clearly labeled fictional case and walk through every step. Skilled trades and restaurant examples work well: a service call, a Saturday-night line.

## 5. Your first win (1 page)
A ten-minute exercise the reader can finish today.

## 6. Checklist (1 page)
The framework as a one-page checklist to print.

## 7. The invitation (1 page)
Who you are in three lines. What the next step is (full product, workshop, coaching). One link. No pressure.

## Back matter
- Copyright line: (c) [YEAR] [YOUR NAME]
- License of any images or fonts used.
- A short disclaimer: educational information, no guarantees of results.
