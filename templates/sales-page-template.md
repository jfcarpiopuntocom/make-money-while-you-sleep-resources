# Sales Page Template

One page, one promise, one button. No hype, no countdown clocks, no income claims, no invented reviews.

## Headline
[The result your reader wants], without [the thing they fear].

## Subheadline
[Product name]: [what it is] for [who it is for].

## The problem
Three short paragraphs in the reader's own words.

## What you get
- [Item 1: what it is and what it does for them]
- [Item 2]
- [Item 3]
- [Bonus: only if it truly helps]

## How it works
1. [Step 1: simple]
2. [Step 2]
3. [Step 3]

## Who it is for / who it is not for
- For: ...
- Not for: ...

## About me
Three honest lines: what you did, for how long, why you teach it. No invented credentials.

## What people say
Only real quotes, with permission. If you have none yet, leave this section out.

## Price and promise
- Price: [PRICE]. One price for everyone.
- Refund promise: [YOUR POLICY IN ONE CLEAR LINE]
- How you receive it: [instant download / email]

## Button
[Get the [PRODUCT] now]

## Questions
- How long does it take?
- What if I get stuck?
- Is it right for beginners?
