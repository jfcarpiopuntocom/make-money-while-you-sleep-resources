# Review the exact package

- The source belongs to us or is permitted for this use.
- Claims match the source; numbers and calculations were checked.
- No invented customer, testimonial, experience or earnings claim appears.
- The product completes the promised task without requiring another purchase.
- The article, script, scenes, cards and captions describe the same product.
- The actual exported video was watched at a realistic phone size.
- Narration, pronunciation and timing were reviewed when audio is present.
- Text has strong contrast; no meaningful label depends on faint gray.
- Image, music, voice and font permissions are recorded.
- The exact final asset hash is saved.
- The destination account, caption and publication settings are identified.
- Approval applies to this asset and destination, with an expiry.
- Publication URL or post ID will be recorded after checking the destination.
- Sources, product files and manifests have a recoverable backup.
