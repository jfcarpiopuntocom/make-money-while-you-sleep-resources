# Offer page worksheet

## What this helps you do

State the task and intended reader in ordinary language.

## See the result

Include a real completed example or demonstration. Label illustrative examples.

## What you receive

List exact files, formats, access conditions and support scope.

## Before you buy

State prerequisites, limits, price, delivery timing and applicable refund terms. Avoid invented urgency and unsupported results.

## Your next step

Give one clear purchase or sample action. The product must work without buying another product.
