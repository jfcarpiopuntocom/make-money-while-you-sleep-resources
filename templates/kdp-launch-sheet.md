## AI-generated content (answer honestly)
KDP asks whether your book contains AI-generated content. Per KDP's help page, AI-generated means text, images or translations created by an AI tool, even if you edited them heavily. AI-assisted means content you wrote yourself and improved with AI for editing, refining or error-checking; that does not need disclosure.

- Is any text AI-generated? Yes / No: ______
- Are any images or the cover AI-generated? Yes / No: ______ (a cover made with an AI tool counts)
- Is any translation AI-generated? Yes / No: ______
- Tool used (you may be asked): ______
Write down your honest answer and keep this page.

## Step 2: Content
- Manuscript file: ______ (EPUB, Word or KPF; preview it in the KDP viewer)
- DRM: yes / no (your choice)
- Cover: upload front cover image ______ (use KDP's current size guidance)
- ISBN (ebook): not required; KDP assigns an ASIN
- Preview the whole book on phone and tablet before moving on

## Step 3: Pricing
- Territories: all territories / selected ______
- Primary marketplace: Amazon.com
- Royalty option: 70% or 35%. The 70% option has a list-price band and a small delivery cost; check the current band on KDP before pricing.
- List price: ______ (no discount codes, no "launch sale" stunts)
- KDP Select (90 days, ebook exclusive to Amazon): yes / no. Decide on purpose; it limits where you can sell the ebook.

## Before you press Publish
- [ ] Title, subtitle and cover say the same promise
- [ ] Spelling checked on the front and back cover and inside
- [ ] No fake reviews, badges or "bestseller" claims
- [ ] Rights to every image and font confirmed
- [ ] Description has no income promises
- [ ] AI-content answers match the truth
