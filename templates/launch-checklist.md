# Launch Checklist

Tick each line. Anything you cannot tick is your next task.

## Product
- [ ] One-sentence promise written
- [ ] Product 0.9 finished and read once out loud
- [ ] Spelling and facts checked; every number sourced or labeled "example"
- [ ] Booklet (free) finished
- [ ] Cover front and back made and checked on a phone screen

## Store and money
- [ ] Store account created (price, description, files)
- [ ] Checkout tested with one real purchase of your own
- [ ] Delivery email arrived and the link works
- [ ] Refund policy written in plain words

## Emails and automations
- [ ] Email tool set up with your sender address
- [ ] F1 imported and tested with your own email
- [ ] Welcome sequence (7 emails) pasted in and tested
- [ ] F3 sale-to-delivery tested
- [ ] Approval Gate (F8) tested: approve once, reject once

## Honesty and safety
- [ ] No income promises, no fake reviews, no earnings screenshots
- [ ] Voice or images used only with rights or consent
- [ ] Nothing private sent to free tools
- [ ] Files backed up in a second place

## Launch day
- [ ] Announce to your first people (personally, not a blast)
- [ ] Watch the first three orders arrive
- [ ] Answer every email within a day

## After
- [ ] Monday dashboard (F7) running
- [ ] Ask buyers for feedback at day 10 (F6)
- [ ] Decide the next offer
