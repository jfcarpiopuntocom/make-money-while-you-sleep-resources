# Safety Rules

Short, plain, and worth reading once.

## The five rules
1. **Passwords and keys stay with you.** Never paste a password, card number, bank detail or API key into a chat, a document or an email. If a tool needs one, you type it yourself into that tool's own settings.
2. **A human approves anything public or paid.** Posts, emails to a list, uploads, purchases and deletions wait for your "yes". The flow F8 (Approval Gate) does this for automations.
3. **Free tools get public work only.** The free helpers in `ai-engine-room/` may log what you send and may use it to train models. Send them public text, never client names, private records or anything confidential.
4. **Back up your work.** Keep a copy of your studio folder somewhere else (a second drive or cloud folder). Before big changes, ask Claude to copy the folder first.
5. **Test with yourself first.** Every automation is tested with your own email address and a fake contact before it touches a real person.

## Also
- Claude Code asks permission before it changes files or runs tools. Read the question before you click yes. If you do not understand it, say "explain that in plain words".
- Do not use a voice that is not yours, or a person's photo, without their written consent.
- No fake reviews, fake testimonials or earnings screenshots. Ever.
- Check the license of any music, image or font before you sell something that contains it.
- If something looks wrong (an email you did not expect, a charge you do not recognize), stop, switch the flow off, and ask Claude to help you review what happened.
